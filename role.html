A little less searching. A little more going out.

The product

What’s happening nearby?

ROLE brings concerts, events, parties and bars into one place for people exploring Porto Alegre. The experience connects an open-ended question — “what should we do?” — with concrete options, dates and places.

My responsibility was the UX/UI of the app. The interfaces cover discovery, the agenda, event details and account flows, with a shared visual language across the experience.

01 / Discovery

Different ways into
the same city.

The revised dashboard brings search, compact categories and six events in a two-column grid into focus. The agenda uses date filters and a single-column list, giving each option more space.

Recommendations focus on the event, venue and time, with one save action. The agenda adds distance for comparison. Original artwork keeps each option recognisable, with fewer competing controls.

02 / Event details

Turn curiosity
into a plan.

The revised event page gives the artwork room to set the mood, then brings the date, time and venue together above a short description.

One primary attendance action makes the next step clear, with saving kept in the header. Social counts, ratings and the tab strip have been removed from the overview; the gallery also includes the related artist and discussion views.

Visual direction

The energy of going out.
The clarity of a familiar interface.

A black canvas lets event photography and posters lead. Purple highlights identify key actions, while rounded controls and compact cards create a consistent rhythm across the app.

The interface uses an informal, conversational voice that connects the product to the way people talk about going out. The screens shown here are presented in English.
